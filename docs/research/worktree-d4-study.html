Decisions

Decision D4.1 · Active indicator + trigger

Where does the active-checkout indicator live, and what opens the switcher?

Your note wants B (display/indicator) prominent. Trade-off: reuse the ribbon (no extra height, but mutates the locked git-ribbon design) vs a dedicated pill (more prominent, +1 row).

Decision D4.2 · Dropdown row content

What does each worktree row in the dropdown show?

The current section shows hue + branch + current flag. The dropdown can afford a bit more (dirty/ahead) since it's transient, not always-on.

Decision D4.3 · Keyboard model

How much keyboard support in v1?

Two existing patterns to mirror: WorkspaceSwitcher dropdown (click-only) or TabSearchPalette (↑↓/Enter/Esc/type-filter).

Decision D4.4 · Keep the bottom-of-tree section too?

Does the dropdown fully replace the bottom-of-tree list, or do both coexist?

You called the bottom list a scrolling problem. But some users like an always-visible list.
