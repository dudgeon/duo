The one soft call is blocked vs question — but since both land in Needs you, a wrong guess only mislabels a reason chip, never the column. That low blast-radius is exactly why merging blocked into Needs you (rather than giving it its own column) is the right call.

Why "planning vs executing" was cut (and what we kept)

We explored a second axis — lifecycle phase (Planning → Executing → Done), readable from plan-mode signals. It's real and deterministic, but it competed with attention for the columns and added a chip most catch-up moments don't need. The owner's call: cut it. The board organizes by attention only. The one piece of plan-mode signal worth keeping is the plan-to-approve reason — which is attention, not phase — so it stays as a Needs-you chip. The raw phase data still lives in the digest if a future view wants it; it's just not surfaced here.

The Done column's action model (owner refinement)

A complete work product does not mean "view the artifact" is the action you want. In most cases you'll want to jump back into the session — to iterate, ask a follow-up, or read the closing message. So the Done cards lead with Open session →, with the work product as a quieter secondary link. Two exceptions and one principle:

  • md / html products lead with the artifact — Open report → opens/focuses it in Duo's canvas (md, source-editable) or playground (html, rendered). This is the one case where the artifact is the thing to look at, and it's Duo's home turf. (Card 3 above.)
  • PRs are a secondary link, not the headline — viewing a PR is the comparatively rare reach, so Review PR #214 sits under the primary, never as it.
  • In Duo these aren't rivals — re-entering a session can bring up the terminal and its canvas side by side (the core pairing), so "Open session" and "Open report" differ only in which one leads.

The planned UI, at a glance

  • Sibling mode toggle — Projects ↔ Catch-up at the top; the app remembers your last choice (persisted app-global, fanned out to every window).
  • Two-tier columns — sessions with an open terminal tab or needing you render as full cards; the rest of the last 7 days (deduped) render as compact rows beneath. A closed session that needs you (e.g. a plan awaiting approval) stays a full card — never demoted to a one-liner.
  • Cron runs are first-class, badged 🕐 scheduled — the canonical "work done while you were away" (a scheduled triage run can even land in Needs you).
  • Three attention columns — Needs you (act) · Working (wait) · Done (review). No phase columns, no group-by toggle.
  • Blocked is a reason chip, not a column — ⛔ blocked sits inside Needs you beside 💬 question and 📋 plan to approve. Three things that all mean "give input," one column.
  • Plan-to-approve is the standout Needs-you case — a proposed plan awaiting your go-ahead (an ExitPlanMode with no approval after it). The crispest "needs you" signal of all, and it was invisible before.
  • Done leads with re-entry — "Open session →" by default; md/html opens the artifact; PR/diff are secondary links (next section).

Decisions

Locked 2026-06-23. Blocked → merged into Needs you as a reason chip. Phase (planning/executing) → cut — attention is the only axis ("get rid of phase, stick to attention"). Plan-to-approve survives as a Needs-you reason. Default grouping → attention (act · wait · review).
