Your two questions reshaped the board — they're the same insight. You couldn't tell blocked from needs-you, and you wondered what planning vs executing gives us. The fix is to recognize there are two independent axes, and my first board conflated them: lifecycle phase (Planning → Executing → Done — what the agent is doing) and attention (does it need you, and why). "Blocked" was never a phase — it's one reason a session needs you. So: one Needs you column with reason chips, and phase becomes its own axis you can group by. The two axes (and the deterministic signal behind each)

① Lifecycle phase — what it's doing

Planning ──▶ Executing ──▶ Done

Where the session is in its arc. Claude Code's plan mode makes this readable: an ExitPlanMode marker or research-only tools (Read/Grep, no Edits yet) = Planning; Edit/Write/Bash calls = Executing; finished + todos complete = Done.

② Attention — does it need you

(cross-cuts every phase)

An interrupt, not a phase — a session can need you while planning (a plan to approve) or while executing (a mid-flight question, or a blocker). That's why "blocked" doesn't deserve its own column: it's just one reason under Needs you.

The Done column's action model (owner refinement)

A complete work product does not mean "view the artifact" is the action you want. In most cases you'll want to jump back into the session — to iterate, ask a follow-up, or read the closing message. So the Done cards lead with Open session →, with the work product as a quieter secondary link. Two exceptions and one principle:

  • md / html products lead with the artifact — Open report → opens/focuses it in Duo's canvas (md, source-editable) or playground (html, rendered). This is the one case where the artifact is the thing to look at, and it's Duo's home turf. (Card 3 above.)
  • PRs are a secondary link, not the headline — viewing a PR is the comparatively rare reach, so Review PR #214 sits under the primary, never as it.
  • In Duo these aren't rivals — re-entering a session can bring up the terminal and its canvas side by side (the core pairing), so "Open session" and "Open report" differ only in which one leads.

What changed from iteration 1

  • Blocked is gone as a column — it's now a red ⛔ blocked reason chip inside Needs you, beside 💬 question and 📋 plan to approve. Three things that all mean "give input," one column.
  • Planning vs executing is a real signal — surfaced as a phase chip on working cards, and as columns when you group by phase. The standout case: a session that's planning AND needs you (a plan awaiting approval) — the cleanest "needs you" of all, and previously invisible.
  • The two axes are separable — the Group-by toggle proves it: attention and phase are orthogonal, so you choose which one organizes your morning.
