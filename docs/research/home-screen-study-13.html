Decisions

Shortlist your favorites, then lock the levers. Mix freely — e.g. "13A base + 13C's briefing line + 13F's terminal only on the lead."

Decision 1 · Hero count & symmetry

How many heroes, and are they equal?

The core lever. Two equal is the baseline; lead+wing spends more on the newest; three widens the rich set but narrows each panel.

Decision 2 · Orientation

Side-by-side, stacked, or responsive?

Home will often be pinned in a split pane beside a terminal, where a side-by-side spread crushes. 13D stacks vertically to survive that.

Decision 3 · Hero richness

What rides inside a hero panel beyond its sessions?

Each step up adds a live JSONL read and a component. The snippet is cheap and evocative; the terminal frame is the strongest cue but the heaviest.

Decision 4 · Voice line

A briefing line on top, or silent?

13C adds one calm line — the salvageable piece of Concierge. It keeps #12's warmth without committing the page to prose.

Decision 5 · The "rest"

How do the non-hero projects render?

The compressed tail still needs to serve cold-project re-entry — the feature's actual job. It must stay scannable and clickable, with a path to "all sessions."
