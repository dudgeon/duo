Decisions

These are tuned to the daring direction. Shortlist 2–4 visions above, then answer — shortlist + answers travel together in the Copy payload.

Decision A · Voice

Does Home speak, or does it just show?

The round-2 visions split sharply: some narrate/recommend (Letter 15, Concierge 24, Notebook 14), most just present. This is the biggest tonal fork — and the one your "#12 is the only creative one" reaction points at.

Decision B · How prominence is earned

What makes the 1–2 hero projects big?

You liked giving recent projects prominence. But "most recent" and "where I most need to re-enter" can differ — the project you touched 12 min ago needs the least help.

Decision C · Content richness

How much of each session do we show beyond its title?

The daring visions bet on richer content — a last-message snippet (13, 19, 24), a frozen terminal frame (18), shared-file threads (23). Each is something only Duo can show, but each costs a JSONL read and a new component.

Decision D · Metaphor commitment

How far do we lean into a physical metaphor?

Notebook (14), Bookshelf (20), Hearth (16), Marquee (22) commit to a real-world object. It's where the warmth lives — and where it can tip twee or fight the calm.

Decision E · The lead vision

If you had to pick one to prototype first, which?

Your shortlist captures favorites; this names the one to build a real, data-backed prototype of next. (Free-text if it's a blend — e.g. "Two-Up frame + Concierge briefing line + snippet.")
