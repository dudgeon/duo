What is Duo?

Duo is a desktop app pairing terminal sessions (where Claude runs) with an embedded browser, a markdown editor, and an interactive HTML canvas. A small CLI bridge — duo — lets Claude drive everything you can see.

Click Start lesson below. Duo will open a fresh Claude tab in your home directory and ask Claude to walk you through the primitives one at a time. You'll click buttons here; Claude will respond by painting the next step into this pane.
